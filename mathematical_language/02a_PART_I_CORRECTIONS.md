| K6a | new | **Proposition 13S (Staircase).** In the staircase representation, $y$ stays at $(j-1)/J$ for the $c_j$ columns of transition $j$ and reaches $j/J$ at knot $C_j$. Sampled at the column lines $k=0,\dots,N_1$, it is affine **if and only if every $c_j=1$**, equivalently $N_1=J$. *Proof.* The samples are non-decreasing from $0$ to $1$. Affine means all $N_1$ increments are equal to $1/N_1>0$. Any $c_j\ge2$ produces a zero increment. If all $c_j=1$, then $y_k=k/J$ with $N_1=J$. ∎ *I guess this is a second exact reading of Statement 5's "traditional music can move from a low row across a single column". Under the staircase, linear means single-column transitions and nothing else.* |
